⚠️ This is a statistical heuristic, not proof. It flags patterns common in synthetic/demo datasets (uniform distributions, equal-weight categories, gapless sequential IDs, poor Benford's Law fit) versus patterns typical of organically collected data. It cannot and does not verify a file's true origin.
